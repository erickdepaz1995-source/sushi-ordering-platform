# Sushi Ordering Platform — Navigation Flow

| | Menu (home) | Product detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Show the available sushi products so customers can quickly explore the menu. | Give the customer more information about a selected product before deciding what to order. |
| **Information shown** | Product name, description, price, category, and availability. | Product name, description, price, category, availability, and additional product details. |
| **Actions** | Browse the menu; open a product to see more. | Review the selected product; go back to the menu. |
| **Navigation** | Comes first when the site opens. Opening a product leads to Product detail. | Reached by opening a product from the menu. "Back to menu" returns to the Menu. |
| **Empty / error state** | "No products are available right now. Please check again soon." | "This product is not available right now. Go back to the menu to explore other options." |
